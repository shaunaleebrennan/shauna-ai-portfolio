**From search to answer to action: making agentic AI easier to buy**

HQ Agent needed a clear story for what it could do, why it mattered and where it fitted within Workvivo's wider AI portfolio.

## Making the value progression clear

Search, grounded answers and task execution can sound like one undifferentiated AI promise. Buyers needed to understand the progression in value, the role of permissions and trust, and how HQ Agent related to Workvivo HQ and ZoomMate.

**The commercial question:** How do search, grounded answers, and supported actions become a progression buyers can understand and teams can sell?

## Connecting technical depth to buying decisions

I led product and portfolio positioning, working with Product and Zoom's AI teams to understand enterprise search, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), grounding, permissions, integrations and supported actions. I turned those capabilities into buyer use cases, seller messaging and enablement for IT, HR and Communications. I also co-developed pricing and packaging with the Revenue leader, considering customer value, AI maturity, use cases, competitive alternatives and sustainable economics.

## What the commercial story needed to establish



- Connect the capability to a use case. Explain what the employee is trying to achieve and how the agent helps.
- Make trust part of the value. Include grounding, permissions, integrations, and the boundaries of supported actions.
- Position within the portfolio. Clarify the relationship between HQ Agent, Workvivo HQ, and ZoomMate.
- Package around customer value. Consider AI maturity, use cases, competitive alternatives, and sustainable economics.

## Search. Answer. Action.

The positioning gave commercial teams a usable progression—search, answer, action—and a more credible explanation of what the product could and could not do. It informed launch and seller enablement and helped connect technical capabilities to commercial value. Internal planning identified a substantial HQ Agent pipeline opportunity, but pipeline estimates and targets are not presented here as achieved or PMM-generated revenue.

> The unit of value in agentic AI is a job completed. That should shape the pitch, the packaging, and the proof.
