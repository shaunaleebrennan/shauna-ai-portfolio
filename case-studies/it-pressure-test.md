**Making a messaging review more useful, sooner**

“This needs to be stronger” is easy feedback to give and hard feedback to use. I wanted a review that helped someone decide what to change next.

## What needed to change

The first version asked reviewers to complete a detailed rubric before receiving useful feedback. I needed to make the review immediately useful for enterprise IT messaging, while accounting for the reader’s remit, buying role and decision stage.

## My part in it

I defined the buyer lenses, review experience and evidence criteria, then used ChatGPT and Codex to prototype and develop the tool. I replaced a manual-first rubric with immediate feedback, priority changes and an editable rewrite brief, and tested how the scoring and source passages behaved.

## What came out of it

A live browser tool that turns positioning judgment into practical editing guidance. It shows the passages behind its feedback and helps the author shape the next draft. The score is an aid to judgment; it does not verify claims or replace a buyer’s response.

> Better messaging starts with understanding the decision your buyer is trying to make.
