# Product launch playbook

**Making deliberate launch decisions as product release cycles accelerate**

I evolved my release-planning template into a practical playbook and discussion tool for PMMs and Product Managers to tier launches, group releases, and plan audience timing.

## Three decisions and three clocks

The playbook connects three practical decisions: how much the change matters, whether related releases create a stronger outcome, and who needs to hear what, when.

## Make the reasoning usable

A six-question discussion tool suggests a provisional tier, grouping approach, audience timing, and open decisions. The PDF playbook explains the reasoning; a separate workbook helps PMM and Product record evidence and agree the plan.
