# Messaging as Code

[← All selected work](../README.md#selected-work)

**Making product messaging a source of truth—not another document to copy**

AI can generate content quickly. Keeping that content accurate, approved and consistent across teams is the harder problem.

## Messaging that can keep up

Sales and Marketing reuse messaging across products, audiences and assets. Each copy-and-paste can weaken the link to its original meaning, supporting evidence or approval. I wanted a system where PMM could maintain the source and teams and AI agents could reuse it reliably.

## Designing for trust and reuse

I led the project, wrote the product messaging with PMM-team support, and defined the content model, approval process, and acceptance criteria. The goal was to give people and AI agents a source they could reuse while keeping PMM ownership and human review clear.

## The workflow changed after testing

I tested AI-assisted authoring and revision workflows, then changed direction when a GitHub-based approach proved less practical for how the team works. The resulting in-house approach uses Zoom agents and Canvas to make messaging easier to find, maintain, and review.

## A completed pilot, with more to build

The HQ Agent pilot is complete and connected to the sales enablement agent, with positive early feedback. Wider message coverage and a design-system-connected content generation workflow are planned, not yet established business outcomes. This is a working example of how I apply hands-on AI knowledge to improve PMM operations—not a claim that the full organisation has adopted it.

> Scaling AI content starts with messaging that people can trust and agents can use.
