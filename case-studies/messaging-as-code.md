**Keeping the story intact as more people and AI reuse it**

AI makes messaging easier to create and distribute. I wanted to make it easier to reuse responsibly, too.

## What needed to change

A message travels through decks, websites, launch kits and AI prompts. Along the way, its meaning, evidence or approval can get lost. I designed an operating model that keeps those connections visible and gives PMMs ownership of the words.

## My part in it

I owned the project, wrote the messaging with support from my PMM team, and defined the content model, approval workflow and acceptance criteria. I directed AI-assisted implementation and tested changes and failures. Through testing, I moved the approach from a GitHub-based workflow to an in-house system using Zoom agents and Canvas, keeping the focus on an authoring experience the team could use.

## What came out of it

The HQ Agent pilot is complete and connected to our sales enablement agent, with positive feedback so far. Phase two is to write and test the remaining messaging. From there, the ambition is an agentic system connected to the design system, bringing governed messaging into content creation and automating more of the workflow.

> The value is in turning messaging into a shared foundation for sales enablement, content creation and automation.
