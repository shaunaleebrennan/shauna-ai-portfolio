# The Meta Migration

[← All selected work](../README.md#selected-work)

**Giving customers confidence when their platform was closing**

Meta’s decision to close Workplace created an urgent choice for customers. They needed a clear path forward, and a reason to feel good about the destination.

## What needed to change

As Meta’s only preferred migration partner, Workvivo faced a major opportunity and a compressed decision window. The story needed to balance urgency with reassurance, while helping sellers stand out in a competitive market.

## My part in it

I shaped migration positioning, competitive differentiation and market messaging, then carried that through seller strategy, enablement and cross-functional GTM coordination. I connected the practical reasons to move with a longer-term case for choosing Workvivo.

## What came out of it

A focused migration story and coordinated go-to-market motion. Forrester later recognised Workvivo’s companywide response to the disruption with its 2026 B2B Return on Integration Honors. I contributed to that programme’s GTM work alongside the wider team.

> Even when customers have to move, the destination still has to earn their choice.
