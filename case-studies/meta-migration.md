# The Meta Migration

[← All selected work](../README.md#selected-work)

**Turning a market disruption into a clear reason to choose Workvivo**

When Meta announced Workplace's closure, its customers had to move. But Workvivo still had to earn their choice.

## What needed to change

Workvivo was named Meta's only preferred migration partner, creating a rare, time-sensitive opportunity. Buyers needed reassurance about moving their communities, evidence that the new platform would meet their needs and a reason to choose Workvivo over competing alternatives.

## My part in it

I led migration positioning, competitive differentiation and seller messaging, connecting the practical migration story to the longer-term value of employee experience. I worked across functions to put the right narrative, objections and enablement into the field during a compressed decision window.

## What came out of it

A coordinated GTM approach that helped the wider business compete for Workplace customers. Workvivo's company-wide response was recognised with Forrester's **2026 B2B Return on Integration Honors**. The award belongs to the cross-functional company effort; my contribution was positioning, competitive strategy and seller enablement.

Internal retrospectives describe substantial company-level customer capture and revenue growth. I have not included those figures here without an approved public source and a clear attribution boundary.
