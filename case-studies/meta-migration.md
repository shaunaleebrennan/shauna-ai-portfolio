# The Meta Migration

[← All case studies and documents](../LIBRARY.md) · [View on the portfolio website](https://shaunaleebrennan.github.io/shauna-ai-portfolio/case-study.html?case=meta-migration)

**Turning disruption into commercial opportunity**

Turning a time-bound market disruption into an award-winning commercial motion—while making migration feel credible, differentiated, and low-risk.

**What this demonstrates:** Migration positioning, competitive differentiation, market messaging, seller strategy, enablement, and fast cross-functional GTM execution.

## Context

Meta announced the closure of Workplace. Workvivo was named Meta’s only preferred migration partner, creating a major, time-bound market opportunity and a highly visible competitive GTM motion.

## The challenge

Workvivo had to move quickly while differentiating against competitors, reducing migration risk, addressing customer uncertainty, enabling sellers, and converting a temporary market event into durable commercial opportunity.

## Strategic insight

The winning story needed to combine urgency with reassurance. Migration could not feel like a technical transfer alone; it needed to feel like a credible path from a closing platform to a stronger long-term employee experience.

## My contribution

- Migration positioning and narrative
- Competitive differentiation
- Seller strategy and enablement
- Market messaging
- Cross-functional coordination of the commercial motion
- Helping translate the market event into a repeatable customer story

## Key decisions

- Lead with a proven, low-risk migration path
- Differentiate the destination—not only the transfer process
- Equip sellers for a compressed, competitive decision cycle
- Balance market urgency with customer confidence

## Outcome

Forrester named Workvivo its 2026 B2B Return on Integration Honors winner for a companywide response to a major market disruption. Shauna’s portfolio identifies the recognised programme as the Meta migration and records her as a significant GTM contributor. The award is a company outcome; no migration-share or ARR result is attributed to Shauna.

## What I learned

When a market window is short, clarity and coordination become competitive advantages. The best displacement story makes both the move and the destination feel compelling.
