# Portfolio Voice Agent

[← All selected work](../README.md#selected-work)

**A portfolio you can have a conversation with**

A case study can tell a story. A conversation can start with the part you actually care about. I wanted my portfolio to do both.

## Start with the visitor’s question

Recruiters, hiring managers and collaborators arrive with different questions. I wanted them to explore the work naturally, with answers that were specific, useful and faithful to my experience.

**The experience to create:** Let visitors begin with their own questions and get answers that stay faithful to the work.

## Designing the conversation

I designed and integrated a voice and text agent on ElevenLabs, with a voice persona based on my own recordings. I configured its personality, prompts, modular knowledge and intent routing, then compared models and tested responses against career and project evidence to improve clarity, relevance and accuracy.

### From a visitor’s question to a grounded answer

Voice or text question → Prompts + intent routing → Curated project knowledge → Voice or text answer

ElevenLabs · Voice persona · Modular knowledge · Response testing

[Read the system overview](../docs/PORTFOLIO_VOICE_AGENT_CASE_STUDY.md)

## The choices behind a useful conversation



- Start with different visitor intents. Recruiters, hiring managers, and collaborators need different routes into the same body of work.
- Design a recognisable voice. Base the voice persona on my recordings and configure its personality and prompts.
- Make knowledge modular. Connect the conversation to curated career and project material through intent routing.
- Test against the experience. Compare models and review answers for clarity, relevance, and accuracy.

## A portfolio you can talk to

A live conversational layer for the portfolio. Visitors can ask about a project, a decision or my approach, while the agent draws on curated career and project material. The work brought voice design, product judgment and hands-on AI configuration together in one experience.

> A portfolio agent earns its place by answering the questions a curated page lets you avoid.
