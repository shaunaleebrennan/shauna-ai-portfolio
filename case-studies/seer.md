**Entering the people-intelligence market**

Building a people-intelligence story around the Listening Gap: the distance between what employees say and what organisations act on.

**What this demonstrates:** Research synthesis, category narrative, thought leadership, product positioning, and a practical GTM frame for employee intelligence.

## Context

Workvivo expanded into People Intelligence with Seer, moving into a buyer and category conversation adjacent to its employee-experience strengths.

## The challenge

Seer could not sound like another survey platform. The story needed to explain how employee feedback moves beyond collection—turning large volumes of qualitative signal into insight that leaders and managers can act on.

## Strategic insight

The narrative became LISTEN → UNDERSTAND → ACT. The Listening Gap gave the strategy a memorable tension: organisations often collect employee feedback, but struggle to convert what they hear into meaningful action.

## My contribution

- Positioning and GTM strategy
- Launch strategy
- Pricing and packaging
- Competitive strategy
- Seller narrative and enablement
- Hiring a dedicated PMM to scale execution while retaining senior strategy ownership

## Key decisions

- Anchor the story in action—not survey collection
- Use AI topic grouping, comment analysis, and sentiment as means to insight
- Connect executive reporting with practical manager action
- Position against established engagement and listening alternatives

## Outcome

Seer established a people-intelligence expansion story spanning employee listening, AI-assisted insight, manager intelligence, and action. No unverified Seer revenue or adoption metrics are claimed.

## What I learned

Adjacent-market entry becomes credible when the narrative explains both the new capability and why the existing brand has earned the right to compete.
