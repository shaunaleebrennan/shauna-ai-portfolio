**Repositioning Workvivo for the AI era**

I led the positioning work behind Workvivo’s shift from an Employee Experience Platform toward an AI-native headquarters for every employee.

## What needed to change

AI was changing how employees find information, use systems and get work done. Workvivo needed to reposition an established Employee Experience Platform for that shift, with a clear point of view on what it should become and why it could credibly compete.

## My part in it

I led strategic positioning and messaging architecture, connecting the HQ platform, HQ Agent and people intelligence in one coherent story. I translated that position for IT, HR and Communications, shaped analyst and competitive framing, and carried it into the website, launch strategy, GTM kits and Sales enablement.

## What came out of it

Workvivo HQ launched with a proposition that brought communication, knowledge, action and people intelligence into a shared headquarters for every employee. The repositioning gave the platform story, product narratives and launch a common strategic direction.

> In enterprise AI, an experience employees already use is a competitive advantage.
