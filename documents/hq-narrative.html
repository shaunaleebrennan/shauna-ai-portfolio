Workvivo HQ

How the category idea connects the buyer problem, product pillars, and launch story.

Market problem

Work had become fragmented across communication, knowledge, action, and employee-intelligence systems. AI risked adding another disconnected destination, particularly for frontline employees.

Strategic reframing

The launch reframed the employee experience platform as a headquarters: one shared place where employees can communicate, find trusted answers, take action, and understand what is happening.

The positioning language used publicly includes:

  • "Every employee deserves a headquarters. Now they have one."
  • "The first AI-native employee experience platform."
  • AI built into the employee experience rather than bolted on.
  • Communication, knowledge, action, and intelligence brought together.

Product architecture

The sales narrative organizes the product around three experience pillars:

  1. Communication and engagement.
  2. Search and knowledge.
  3. People intelligence.

HQ Agent extends the story from finding answers to reasoning, recommended next steps, and completing tasks.

Go-to-market system evidenced

  • Product and category narrative.
  • GTM kits built and Sales enabled for the Workvivo HQ launch.
  • Launch website.
  • Organic social launch sequence and carousel.
  • Product UI and capability messaging.
  • Analyst-recognition proof.
