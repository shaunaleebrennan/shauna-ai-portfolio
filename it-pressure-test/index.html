      What this checks

      Six IT buyer questions

      A test of whether the message helps an IT buyer understand, evaluate or defend a technology decision.

      • What business problem forces action?
      • How does this affect the wider system?
      • Can I defend the value internally?
      • What will implementation really require?
      • What evidence reduces the risk?
      • Is the message specific and honest?
      Stage-aware: Discover content should define the problem and consequences. Evaluate content must explain fit and provide proof. Commit content must support financial justification, implementation confidence and consensus.
      Research behind the lens

      Six IT remits and three buying roles guide the questions. Scores count language signals using prototype weights, not validated buyer preferences. Supplied proof is retained as context and is not verified. Research statistics from the source prototype are withheld pending source verification.
