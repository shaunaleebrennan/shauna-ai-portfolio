      Six IT buyer questions

      Use the buyer’s responsibilities to shape a relevant message.

      • What problem makes this worth attention?
      • What work improves, and how?
      • How does it fit the existing stack?
      • What controls and boundaries apply?
      • What supports the central promise?
      • Is the idea easy to repeat?
      Stage-aware: Discover content earns attention. Evaluate content explains fit and support. Commit content helps justify the decision.
      About the method

      A general IT lens and six specialist remits guide the questions. Scores use explicit 0–4 structural checks and editorial format-and-stage weights. They are not calibrated against buyer outcomes.

      The tool runs locally with no model calls, automatic saving or analytics. Supplied evidence is unverified. Human review remains necessary.
