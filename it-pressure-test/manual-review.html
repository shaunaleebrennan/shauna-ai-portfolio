01 / Principle

A diagnostic, not market validation.

The score helps prioritise questions and revisions. It does not prove message-market fit; only customer and performance evidence can do that.

Local

03

Score the eight dimensions

Use 1–5 anchors, cite the source, record what is missing, then make one focused recommendation. Responsible AI may be marked N/A when no AI claim is present.

1 Missing or misleading 3 Adequate but incomplete 5 Clear and well supported

The review dimensions are preparing…

Each dimension requires a score or not-applicable selection, supporting evidence, missing context, confidence, and a recommendation.
