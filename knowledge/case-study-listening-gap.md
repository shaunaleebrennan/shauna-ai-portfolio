# The Listening Gap

## Communication job

Turn employee-listening research into a sharp category narrative that shows why existing programs fail frontline employees and what leaders should do differently.

## Narrative structure

The report introduces a "Listening Funnel" with three stages:

1. Inclusion - who is invited to give feedback.
2. Leadership accountability - who takes ownership.
3. Visible impact - whether employees see change.

Its central argument is that inclusion in listening programs is uneven, leadership accountability is inconsistent, organizations fail to close the loop, and surveys are often not designed for frontline employees.

## Action layer

The report turns findings into five recommendations:

1. Design for every employee.
2. Take visible action.
3. Hold leaders accountable.
4. Close the loop consistently.
5. Break down feedback silos.

## Why it belongs in the portfolio

This artifact demonstrates the structure of research-led product marketing: define a memorable framework, communicate evidence visually, move from problem to implication, and connect the category story to a practical action system.
