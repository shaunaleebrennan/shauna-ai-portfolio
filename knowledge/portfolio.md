# Career and leadership

Shauna Azevedo-Brennan is a Product Marketing Leader focused on complex B2B products, enterprise AI, and category strategy. Based in Dublin, she connects technical product understanding with positioning, launches, pricing, packaging, and seller enablement.

This is a public career summary, aligned with the published résumé. Individual role and contribution statements are professional self-report; company milestones are company context. See the [claims and sources](../docs/EVIDENCE_REGISTER.md).

## Experience and progression

- **Workvivo by Zoom, October 2022–present:** Product Marketing Manager → Senior Product Marketing Manager → Lead Product Marketing Manager → Acting Head of Product Marketing. Founding product marketer; built the first PMM function into a five-person team.
- **Diligent, May 2021–September 2022:** Product Marketing Manager. Owned EMEA GTM for governance, risk, and compliance technology serving executive, board, and IT audiences.
- **Glofox, November 2019–May 2021:** Founding product marketer, building positioning, messaging, launch, and customer-insight foundations.
- **Dencan Restaurants, June 2017–December 2018:** Marketing Manager. Led multi-brand campaigns and managed more than $2.4M in marketing investment. This is managed investment, not generated revenue.

## Leadership and commercial scope

At Workvivo, Shauna leads portfolio positioning and GTM across Workvivo HQ, HQ Agent, and Seer. Her scope includes launches, pricing and packaging, analyst strategy, competitive intelligence, and seller enablement, working across Product, Engineering, Revenue, and Marketing. She contributes to executive strategy and planning, coaches the five-person team through organisational change, and sets messaging direction for Marketing and Growth campaigns, SEO/AEO, launches, demos, and sales enablement.

She owns the analyst relations program, including executive briefings, strategy days, evidence, evaluations, and events. This includes supporting four consecutive Gartner Magic Quadrant Leader placements and Workvivo’s 2026 Forrester Wave Leader position, plus invitations to Employee Experience Management and Cognitive Search Wave evaluations, both in progress. These are company recognitions and independent evaluations.

She also owns the competitive intelligence program, replacing Klue with Steve AI connected to seller tools, and serves as a market intelligence partner to executive leadership.

During her tenure, Workvivo grew from approximately $10M to approximately $110M ARR, reached 9 million users, and became part of Zoom. These are company milestones, not results attributed to Shauna alone. The evidence register distinguishes the externally sourced milestone above $100M from the approved CV’s $110M company context.
